# Health integration roadmap

Private check-ins remain stored as an opt-in, athlete-controlled data feature.
They are deliberately not shown in the current athlete navigation and they never
automatically alter a training plan.

Before any future health-data integration, the product needs explicit consent,
clear data retention/deletion controls, role-based coach access, an auditable
recommendation policy, and clinical/privacy review. Any future plan guidance
must be explainable, reversible, and presented for athlete or coach acceptance.
