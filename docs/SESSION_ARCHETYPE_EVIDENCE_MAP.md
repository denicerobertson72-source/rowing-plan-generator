# Session archetype evidence map

All session names, notes and parameter envelopes are original app wording.
They are not copied protocols. Source IDs identify the evidence concept that
informed the category, while the exact ranges remain versioned app coaching
rules.

| Source IDs | Used for | Not claimed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S010, S011 | Variety, periodized low-intensity emphasis, general-to-specific progression | A universal intensity distribution or fixed session recipe |
| S014, S015, S016 | Bounded high-intensity, transport, anaerobic and sprint categories | A copied intervention protocol or universal dose |
| S017 | Strength/endurance coexistence and conservative same-day metadata | Exact recovery hours or a lifting program |
| S018, S019, S020 | Mode-specific accounting, RPE/recovery context, threshold caution | Exact physiology from generic bands |
| S009 | Taper-compatible low-cost roles | A template-level taper transformation |
| S012, S013, S026 | 2k and short-power relevance | A predicted 2k or sex-specific intensity reduction |

No archetype reduces targets or duration because of sex, age, menopause status,
or menstrual phase. Masters and female athletes are represented through their
demonstrated workload, recovery and coaching context in later selection logic.
